A cube of side 10 between the origin and (10, 10, 10), and the ball of radius 5 centred at the
cube's centre, (5, 5, 5). The ball is inscribed: it touches each of the cube's six faces at a
single point, the middle of the face, and lies everywhere else strictly inside the cube.

Make the solid common to both. It is the whole ball: the six points where the sphere touches the
cube's faces cut nothing from it.
