A ball of radius 5 centred on the origin, united with a cylinder of the same radius on the z
axis, from z = −3 to z = 3.

Between z = −3 and z = 3 the ball lies inside the cylinder and touches its wall from inside along
the equator, the circle of radius 5 in the xy plane, where the sphere and the wall are tangent.
The union is the cylinder with a spherical cap of height 2 on each end. Each cap stands on a
ledge, the ring of its end face between radius 4 and radius 5. The equator is not an edge of the
result: the wall runs through it unbroken.
