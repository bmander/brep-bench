A cube of side 10 between the origin and (10, 10, 10), with a saw cut a millionth wide down into
it from the top. The slot is the block between x = 5 and x = 5.000001, running right through the
cube in y, from y = 0 to y = 10, and from z = 5 up through the top face. If you cut it with a
block, make that block run past the cube's front, back and top.

The slot's two walls, 10 × 5 each, are 10⁻⁶ apart. They are two faces of the result, and so are
the slot's floor and the two halves of the top face.
