A solid torus about the z axis, the middle circle of its tube of radius 10 in the xy plane and
the tube of radius 3, united with a cylinder (a drum) of radius 13 on the z axis, from z = −1 to
z = 1.

The torus reaches out to radius 13 only at its outer equator, the circle of radius 13 in the xy
plane, and there it touches the drum's wall from inside, the two surfaces tangent along the whole
circle. The torus is thicker than the drum: it comes out through each of the drum's flat faces in
the ring between radius 10 − √8 and 10 + √8. The union is the drum with half of the torus's tube
standing out of each face. The outer equator is not an edge of the result: the drum's wall runs
through it unbroken.
