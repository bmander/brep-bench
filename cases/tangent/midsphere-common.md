A cube of side 10 centred on the origin, from (−5, −5, −5) to (5, 5, 5), and a ball centred on
the origin of radius 5√2, the distance from the centre to the middle of an edge. The sphere
touches each of the cube's twelve edges at its midpoint, from inside the edge's two faces, and
the cube's eight corners stick out of the ball.

Make the solid common to both. Each face of the cube cuts the sphere in the circle inscribed in
that face, so each face of the result is the disc of radius 5 inscribed in a face of the cube,
and the discs of neighbouring faces touch at the midpoint of the edge between them. The rest of
the surface is the sphere, left in eight three-sided patches, one over each corner of the cube,
which touch one another only at those same twelve points. The solid is one piece and its surface
is a manifold: at each edge midpoint two discs and two sphere patches meet at a point,
alternating round it.
