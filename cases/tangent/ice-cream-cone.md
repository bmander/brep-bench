A ball of radius 15 centred on the origin, united with a solid cone on the z axis whose apex is
at (0, 0, −25) and whose flat base is the disc of radius 12 in the plane z = −9, 16 above the
apex.

The cone's side is tangent to the sphere along the rim of its base, the circle of radius 12 at
z = −9: every line from the apex up the cone's side touches the sphere there. The base disc lies
inside the ball, its rim on the sphere. The union is an ice-cream cone: the cone's side from the
apex up to that circle, and above it the sphere, the two meeting along the circle with a common
tangent plane.
