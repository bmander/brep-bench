A capsule's end: a cylinder of radius 5 on the z axis from z = 0 to z = 10, capped by the half ball
of radius 5 centred on (0, 0, 10), so that its top is a hemisphere joined to the side tangentially
along the circle z = 10. Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open at its hemisphere. The outside of
the wall is its side and its base, unmoved, and the inside is each of them moved 1 inward along its
normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces are extended or trimmed where they meet
one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an opening, which is left as a rim between
the outside and the inside.

The opening is tangent to the wall it meets. The side, moved 1 inward, is the cylinder of radius 4,
and it runs on past the end of the side's own face, z = 10, up into the hemisphere, until it meets
the sphere of radius 5 about (0, 0, 10) in the circle of radius 4 at z = 13. The bottom, moved, is
the plane z = 1. So the cavity is the cylinder of radius 4 from z = 1 up to z = 13 together with the
cap of the ball above z = 13, and the rim is the zone of the sphere between z = 10 and z = 13, a
band whose inner edge, at z = 13, is a sharp lip where it meets the inside wall.
