The part of the ball of radius 5 centred on the origin below the plane z = 3. Shell it inward to a
wall 1 thick, open at its flat face, z = 3. The outside of the wall is its sphere, unmoved, and the
inside is the sphere moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved
faces are extended or trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the surface of
an opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

The flat face is the disc of radius 4 at z = 3. The sphere, moved 1 inward, is the sphere of radius
4 about the origin, which runs on until it meets the plane z = 3 in the circle of radius √7. So the
result is the part of the ball of radius 5 below z = 3 less the part of the ball of radius 4 below
it, its rim the annulus between radii √7 and 4 at z = 3. Neither sphere meets the plane at right
angles, and the inside one meets it at a different angle from the outside one, so the rim is not the
outside wall's end moved straight in.
