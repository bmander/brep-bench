The block from the origin to (20, 20, 10) with a hole of radius 3 bored right through it along z,
its axis the line x = 10, y = 10. Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open at its top face, z = 10.
The outside of the wall is the block's other faces, the bore among them, unmoved, and the inside is
each of them moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces are
extended or trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an
opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

The bore's wall faces the axis, so moving it inward, into the material, makes it larger: it is the
cylinder of radius 4 on the same axis. The result is a cup with a tube standing in it: the drilled
block less the box from (1, 1, 1) to (19, 19, 10) outside the cylinder of radius 4. The tube is the
annulus between radii 3 and 4, and the bottom, from z = 0 to z = 1, keeps the hole of radius 3; the
rim is the top less the hole and the cavity's mouth.
