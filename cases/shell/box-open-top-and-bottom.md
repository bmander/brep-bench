The box from the origin to (10, 8, 6). Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open at its top face, z =
6, and its bottom face, z = 0. The outside of the wall is the box's other four faces, unmoved, and
the inside is each of them moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The
moved faces are extended or trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the
surface of an opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

With two openings opposite each other the cavity runs right through: the result is a rectangular
tube, the box less the box from (1, 1, 0) to (9, 7, 6), with a hole through it (genus 1) and a rim
at each end.
