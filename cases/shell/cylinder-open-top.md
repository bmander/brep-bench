A cylinder of radius 5 on the z axis, from z = 0 to z = 10. Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open
at its top face, z = 10. The outside of the wall is its side and its bottom, unmoved, and the inside
is each of them moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces
are extended or trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an
opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

The side, moved 1 inward, is the cylinder of radius 4 on the same axis, and the bottom is the plane
z = 1: the result is the cylinder less the cylinder of radius 4 from z = 1 to z = 10, a cup, its rim
the annulus between radii 4 and 5 at z = 10.
