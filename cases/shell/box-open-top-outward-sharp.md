The box from the origin to (10, 8, 6), shelled outward to a wall 1 thick, open at its top face, z =
6, with its edges kept sharp. The box's faces other than the top become the inside of the wall; the
outside is each of them moved 1 outward along its normal, the moved faces extended until they meet,
and cut off by the plane of the top, z = 6, which is the rim.

The result is the box from (−1, −1, −1) to (11, 9, 6) less the box from the origin to (10, 8, 6).
