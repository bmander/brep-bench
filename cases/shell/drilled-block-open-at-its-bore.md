The block from the origin to (20, 20, 10) with a hole of radius 3 bored right through it along z,
its axis the line x = 10, y = 10. Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open at the bore, its
cylindrical face of radius 3. The outside of the wall is the block's six flat faces, unmoved, and
the inside is each of them moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The
moved faces are extended or trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the
surface of an opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

All six of the block's faces are walls, moved 1 inward, and the cavity, the box from (1, 1, 1) to
(19, 19, 9), runs out to the bore's cylinder: the result is a hollow box whose cavity opens into the
hole. The rim lies on the cylinder of radius 3, in two bands each 1 high, from z = 0 to z = 1 and
from z = 9 to z = 10, and the inside top and bottom each have the hole of radius 3 through them.
