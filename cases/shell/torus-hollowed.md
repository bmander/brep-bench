A solid torus about the z axis, centred on the origin, whose tube has radius 2 about a middle circle
of radius 6. Hollow it to a wall 0.5 thick with no opening: the torus stays as the outside, and
moved 0.5 inward it is the inside, the torus whose tube has radius 1.5 about the same middle circle.
The result is the torus less that torus, one solid bounded by two tori, the inner one facing in,
towards the void, which goes round the hole.
