The box from the origin to (20, 14, 10) with its four vertical edges rounded to radius 2: a plate
whose outline is the 20 × 14 rectangle with its corners rounded to radius 2, 10 high. Shell it
inward to a wall 3 thick, open at its top face, z = 10. The outside of the wall is the plate's other
faces, its rounds among them, unmoved, and the inside is each of them moved 3 inward along its
normal, onto the surface offset 3 from it. The moved faces are extended or trimmed where they meet
one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an opening, which is left as a rim between
the outside and the inside.

The thickness is more than the rounds' radius. A round moved 3 inward would be a cylinder of radius
2 − 3 = −1, turned inside out: it is no part of the result, and the inside walls on either side of
it, moved, run on past it until they meet in a sharp edge. So the cavity is the sharp-cornered box
from (3, 3, 3) to (17, 11, 10). The wall is every point of the plate within 3 of its faces other
than the top, and is 3 thick everywhere but across its corners, where it is thicker.
