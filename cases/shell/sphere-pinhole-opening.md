The part of the ball of radius 5 centred on the origin below the plane z = 3.9999. Shell it inward
to a wall 1 thick, open at its flat face, z = 3.9999. The outside of the wall is its sphere,
unmoved, and the inside is the sphere moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1
from it. The moved faces are extended or trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they
meet the surface of an opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

The sphere, moved 1 inward, is the sphere of radius 4 about the origin, whose top, z = 4, is only
0.0001 above the plane: it meets the plane in a circle of radius √(16 − 3.9999²) ≈ 0.028, a pinhole
through which the cavity opens. The result is the part of the ball of radius 5 below z = 3.9999 less
the part of the ball of radius 4 below it, its rim the annulus at z = 3.9999 between that pinhole
and the outer radius √(25 − 3.9999²). (An annulus between two circles cut from concentric spheres by
one plane has the area π(5² − 4²) = 9π, whatever the plane.)
