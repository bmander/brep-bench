A cylinder of radius 5 on the z axis, from z = 0 to z = 10. Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open
at its curved side. The outside of the wall is its two end faces, unmoved, and the inside is each of
them moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces are
extended or trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an
opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

Only the two ends are walls. Moved 1 inward they are the planes z = 1 and z = 9, and each runs out
to the cylinder's surface, where its rim is a band 1 high. Nothing joins them: the result is two
separate solids, the discs of radius 5 from z = 0 to z = 1 and from z = 9 to z = 10.
