The box from the origin to (10, 8, 6). Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open at its top face, z =
6. The outside of the wall is the box's other five faces, unmoved, and the inside is each of them
moved 1 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces are extended or
trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an opening, which is
left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

The result is a box open at the top, the textbook shell: the box less the box from (1, 1, 1) to (9,
7, 6). Its eleven faces are the five outside walls, the five inside walls, and the rim at z = 6, a
rectangular frame 1 wide.
