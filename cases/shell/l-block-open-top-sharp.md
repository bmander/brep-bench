An L-shaped plate: its outline in the xy plane is the polygon through (0, 0), (20, 0), (20, 5), (5,
5), (5, 15) and (0, 15), extruded 8 along +z. Its one concave edge is the vertical line x = 5, y =
5. Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open at its top face, z = 8. The outside of the wall is the
plate's other faces, unmoved, and the inside is each of them moved 1 inward along its normal, onto
the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces are extended or trimmed where they meet one another,
and run on until they meet the surface of an opening, which is left as a rim between the outside and
the inside. Here every pair of moved faces is extended until they meet, the concave edge included,
so that no round is added.

The two walls at the concave edge, x = 5 and y = 5, moved 1 inward, are x = 4 and y = 4, and
extended they meet in the line x = 4, y = 4: the wall at the inside corner is thicker than 1 across
the corner, √2 from the edge to the line. The cavity is the polygon through (1, 1), (19, 1), (19,
4), (4, 4), (4, 14) and (1, 14), from z = 1 up to the top.
