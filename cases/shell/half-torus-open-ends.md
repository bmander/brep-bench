The half with y ≥ 0 of the solid torus about the z axis, centred on the origin, whose tube has
radius 2 about a middle circle of radius 6. It has two flat faces, the discs of radius 2 about (6,
0, 0) and (−6, 0, 0) in the plane y = 0. Shell it inward to a wall 0.5 thick, open at both flat
faces. The outside of the wall is its torus face, unmoved, and the inside is the torus face moved
0.5 inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 0.5 from it. The moved faces are extended or
trimmed where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an opening, which is
left as a rim between the outside and the inside.

The torus, moved 0.5 inward, is the torus whose tube has radius 1.5, and the result is a curved
pipe: the half torus less the half torus of tube radius 1.5, its rims two annuli between radii 1.5
and 2 at its ends.
