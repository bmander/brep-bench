A capsule's end: a cylinder of radius 5 on the z axis from z = 0 to z = 10, capped by the half ball
of radius 5 centred on (0, 0, 10), so that its top is a hemisphere joined to the side tangentially
along the circle z = 10. Shell it inward to a wall 1 thick, open at its base, the disc at z = 0. The
outside of the wall is its side and its hemisphere, unmoved, and the inside is each of them moved 1
inward along its normal, onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces are extended or trimmed
where they meet one another, and run on until they meet the surface of an opening, which is left as
a rim between the outside and the inside.

The side, moved 1 inward, is the cylinder of radius 4, and the hemisphere is the hemisphere of
radius 4 about the same centre; they still meet tangentially, along the circle of radius 4 at z =
10. The result is the capsule less the capsule of radius 4 from z = 0, its rim the annulus between
radii 4 and 5 at z = 0.
