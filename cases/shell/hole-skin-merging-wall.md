The block from the origin to (20, 20, 10) with a hole of radius 3 bored right through it along z,
its axis the line x = 15.5, y = 10, so that the hole comes within 1.5 of the side x = 20. Shell it
inward to a wall 1 thick, open at its top face, z = 10. The outside of the wall is the block's other
faces, the bore among them, unmoved, and the inside is each of them moved 1 inward along its normal,
onto the surface offset 1 from it. The moved faces are extended or trimmed where they meet one
another, and run on until they meet the surface of an opening, which is left as a rim between the
outside and the inside.

The bore, moved 1 inward, is the cylinder of radius 4 about its axis, which reaches x = 19.5, past
the inside wall x = 19: the tube round the bore runs into the wall and the two merge. The cavity is
the box from (1, 1, 1) to (19, 19, 10) outside the cylinder of radius 4; the cylinder meets the
inside wall x = 19 in two vertical lines at y = 10 ± √15/2, and the arc of it beyond x = 19, an
angle 2·acos(7/8) wide, is no part of the result.
