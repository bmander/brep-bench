A solid horn torus centred on the origin, about the z axis: the circle through the middle of its
tube has radius 5, and the tube has radius 5 too. It is every point within 5 of that circle, the
disc (x − 5)² + z² ≤ 25 of the xz plane turned a whole turn about the z axis.

The tube's radius equals the middle circle's, so the tube closes its hole to a single point: the
origin, where the disc touches the axis. There the surface pinches, every meridian's circle
passing through it: what was the hole is two dimples, above and below, each a cusp narrowing to
the axis (its radius about z²/10 near the origin), whose tips meet there. It is one solid, with no
hole through it.
