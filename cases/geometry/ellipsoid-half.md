The ellipsoid x²/36 + y²/16 + z²/9 ≤ 1 (semi-axes 6 along x, 4 along y and 3 along z, centred on
the origin), with everything below the plane z = 0 cut away. What is left is its upper half: a
dome standing on the flat ellipse x²/36 + y²/16 ≤ 1 in the plane z = 0, rising to 3 at its top.

The cut runs through the ellipsoid's widest section, where its surface stands upright, so the dome
meets its floor at a right angle all round. If you cut with a block, make it reach past the
ellipsoid everywhere but at z = 0.
