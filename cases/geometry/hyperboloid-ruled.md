The solid bounded by two flat discs and the surface ruled between their rims, the rims turned a
quarter turn against each other.

The two rims are circles of radius 5 about the z axis, one in the plane z = −5 and one in
z = 5. Join the point at angle θ on the lower circle, (5 cos θ, 5 sin θ, −5), by a straight line to
the point at angle θ + 90° on the upper one, (5 cos(θ + 90°), 5 sin(θ + 90°), 5), for every θ. Those
lines sweep the solid's side; the discs the circles bound are its ends.

The side is a hyperboloid of one sheet, x² + y² = 25/2 + z²/2: its waist, in the plane z = 0, has
radius 5/√2, where each line passes nearest the axis. Build the side as a ruled surface, as the
lines are given, rather than as a hyperbola turned.
