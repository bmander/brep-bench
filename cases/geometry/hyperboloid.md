The solid inside a hyperboloid of one sheet about the z axis, between the planes z = −6 and z = 6:
every point with x² + y² ≤ 16(1 + z²/36) and −6 ≤ z ≤ 6. Its waist, in the plane z = 0, is a circle
of radius 4; it flares to a circle of radius 4√2 at each end, which is flat.

Its side is the hyperbola x²/16 − z²/36 = 1 of the xz plane (its branch with x > 0, between
z = −6 and z = 6) turned a whole turn about the z axis. Its curvature is negative everywhere: it
curves away from its axis along each meridian and round it along each parallel.
