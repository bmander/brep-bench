A sheet: five faces of the cube from the origin to (10, 10, 10), its floor at z = 0 and its four
walls, with no top: an open tray, its boundary the square of the walls' top edges at z = 10. Thicken
it by 1 into the cube. The moved faces cross near the tray's edges and corners, and each is trimmed
where it meets the others.

The thickened sheet is the cube less the box from (1, 1, 1) to (9, 9, 10): a tray with walls and a
floor 1 thick, its rim the flat square ring between the squares of side 10 and 8 at z = 10.
