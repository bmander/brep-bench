A sheet of two flat faces meeting at a right angle along a crease: the rectangle in the xy plane
from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 8, 0), the floor, and the rectangle in the yz plane from (0, 0, 0) to
(0, 8, 10), the wall. They share the edge from (0, 0, 0) to (0, 8, 0).

Thicken it by 1 to the outside of the crease, below the floor and behind the wall (to −z and −x),
with the corner rounded: each face moves 1 along its normal, and the gap the moved faces leave
between them at the crease is closed by the quarter of the cylinder of radius 1 about the crease
that lies between them.

The thickened sheet's section in any plane y = const is the floor's strip from z = −1 to 0, the
wall's strip from x = −1 to 0, and the quarter disc of radius 1 about the origin with x ≤ 0 and
z ≤ 0 between them, extruded from y = 0 to y = 8.
