A sheet: the torus about the z axis, centred on the origin, whose tube has radius 2 about a
middle circle of radius 6, and of its surface only the inner half: the points of it no farther
than 6 from the z axis. It is one face, saddle-shaped everywhere, bounded by the circles of radius
6 at z = 2 and z = −2, the top and bottom of the tube. Thicken it by 1 away from the middle circle,
toward the z axis.

The thickened sheet's section through the axis, on one side of it, is the half annulus between
radii 2 and 3 about the point at distance 6 from the axis, z = 0, on the axis's side of it. Its
faces are the sheet, the inner half of the torus of tube radius 3 it moves to, and the two
cylinders of radius 6 that its boundary circles sweep, from z = 2 to 3 and from z = −2 to −3.
