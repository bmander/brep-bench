A sheet: the half of the cylinder of radius 5 about the z axis where y ≥ 0, from z = 0 to
z = 10. It is one curved face, bounded by two half circles, at z = 0 and z = 10, and two straight
lines, at (5, 0) and (−5, 0). Thicken it by 1 away from the axis.

The thickened sheet is the half of the thick-walled tube between radii 5 and 6 where y ≥ 0, from
z = 0 to z = 10. Its faces are the sheet, the half cylinder of radius 6 it moves to, a half annulus
at each end, and the two flat strips, each 1 by 10, that its straight edges sweep in the plane
y = 0.
