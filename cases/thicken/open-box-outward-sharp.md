A sheet: five faces of the cube from the origin to (10, 10, 10), its floor at z = 0 and its four
walls, with no top: an open tray, its boundary the square of the walls' top edges at z = 10. Thicken
it by 1 out of the cube, with its edges kept sharp: each face moves 1 along its normal, and the
moved faces, which part at the tray's edges, are extended until they meet, so that no round is
added.

The thickened sheet is the box from (−1, −1, −1) to (11, 11, 10) less the cube: a tray with walls
and a floor 1 thick, its rim the flat square ring between the squares of side 12 and 10 at
z = 10.
