A sheet of two flat faces meeting at a right angle along a crease: the rectangle in the xy plane
from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 8, 0), the floor, and the rectangle in the yz plane from (0, 0, 0) to
(0, 8, 10), the wall. They share the edge from (0, 0, 0) to (0, 8, 0).

Thicken it by 1 to the inside of the crease, above the floor and in front of the wall (to +z and
+x). The moved faces cross near the crease, and each is trimmed where it meets the other.

The thickened sheet's section in any plane y = const is the L through (0, 0), (10, 0), (10, 1),
(1, 1), (1, 10) and (0, 10), each given as (x, z), extruded from y = 0 to y = 8: the floor's and
the wall's strips, overlapping in the square 1 by 1 at the crease.
