A sheet: the half of the cylinder of radius 5 about the z axis where y ≥ 0, from z = 0 to
z = 10, one curved face bounded by two half circles and two straight lines. Thicken it by 2
toward the axis: to the sheet's concave side, where its moved face is smaller than itself.

The thickened sheet is the half of the thick-walled tube between radii 3 and 5 where y ≥ 0, from
z = 0 to z = 10. Its faces are the sheet, the half cylinder of radius 3 it moves to, a half annulus
at each end, and two flat strips, each 2 by 10, in the plane y = 0.
