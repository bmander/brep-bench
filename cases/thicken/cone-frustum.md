A sheet: the side of the cone frustum about the z axis whose radius runs from 6 at z = 0 to 3
at z = 4 (5 along its slant), one face bounded by those two circles. Thicken it by 1 along its
normal away from the axis. That normal leans up: in a section through the axis it is (4/5, 3/5),
4/5 outward and 3/5 up.

The thickened sheet's section through the axis, on one side of it, is the rectangle 5 by 1 with
corners (6, 0), (3, 4), (3.8, 4.6) and (6.8, 0.6), each given as (distance from the axis, z). Its
faces are the sheet, the frustum it moves to (radius 6.8 at z = 0.6 to 3.8 at z = 4.6), and two
rims that are neither flat nor cylinders: each rim's circle sweeps a frustum of a cone, the bottom
one from radius 6 at z = 0 to 6.8 at z = 0.6, the top one from 3 at z = 4 to 3.8 at z = 4.6.
