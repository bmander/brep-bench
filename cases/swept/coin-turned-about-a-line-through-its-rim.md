The solid a thick disc fills as it is turned partway about an upright line that passes through its
rim.

The disc is a solid cylinder of radius 2 whose axis is the line through (2, 0, 0) parallel to the y
axis, from y = 0 to y = 0.5, its faces flat. Its rim in the plane y = 0, a sharp circle of radius 2,
passes through the origin, on the z axis.

It turns rigidly about the z axis, right-handed (from +x toward +y), by 90°. Make the solid of
everything the disc passes through.

Each rim is a sharp circle leaning to the turn's velocity; the one through the axis sweeps a surface
of revolution that closes on the axis, and the disc's flat face there turns about a line in its own
plane.
