A pipe of circular section, radius 1, following a helix about the z axis. The helix has radius 10 and rises 5 per turn. It starts at (10, 0, 0), turns right-handed (from +x towards +y) and makes three whole turns. The section stays square to the path. Each end is closed by a half ball of radius 1, as a ball rolled along the helix would leave.
