The rod and tunnel of `swept/box-screwed-inside-a-rod`, a hundred times larger, 2 m long if a unit
is a millimetre: a rod, the cylinder of radius 500 about the z axis from z = 0 to z = 2000, with a
sealed helical tunnel inside it: everything a box passes through as it is carried along a screw
wholly within the rod.

The box, as placed, is 250 ≤ x ≤ 350, −50 ≤ y ≤ 50, 200 ≤ z ≤ 300.

The screw is about the z axis: the box turns right-handed about it (from +x toward +y) and rises
400 for every whole turn, rigidly, from a turn of 0° to one of 720°, ending as the box
250 ≤ x ≤ 350, −50 ≤ y ≤ 50, 1000 ≤ z ≤ 1100. Cut from the rod everything the box passes through.

The box stays inside the rod all the way, so the result is the rod with a closed helical cavity two
turns long, square in section and ended by the box's own faces: two shells. The box's level faces
sweep helicoidal sheets and its upright faces cylinders and ruled sheets, meeting along its edges'
helices.
