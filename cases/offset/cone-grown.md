A cone on the z axis with a base of radius 3 at z = 0 and its apex at (0, 0, 4), grown by 1: the
solid of every point within 1 of the cone.

The base moves down to z = −1, and the cone's side moves 1 outward to a cone of the same angle,
whose apex is 1/sin α = 5/3 higher, α = atan(3/4) being the half-angle at the apex. The base's rim,
where the side meets the base at an angle of β = atan(4/3) inside the cone, becomes a piece of a
torus's tube of radius 1 about the rim's circle, turning through π − β. The apex becomes a cap of
a ball of radius 1 about it, which meets the moved side along a circle of radius 0.8 at z = 4.6.
