A napkin ring: a ball of radius 5 centred on the origin, with a hole of radius 3 bored right
through it along the z axis. Grow it by 1: the solid of every point within 1 of the ring.

The ring's outer face moves out to the sphere of radius 6, and its bore narrows to radius 2. Each
of its rims, the circle of radius 3 at z = 4 or z = −4 where the sphere meets the bore at an angle
of θ = atan(4/3) (about 53.13°) inside the ring, becomes a piece of a torus's tube of radius 1
about the rim's circle, turning through π − θ (about 126.87°) from the bore to the sphere: from
z = ±4 on the bore to the point (3.6, ±4.8) of a section through the axis.
