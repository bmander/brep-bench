A drum: the cylinder of radius 10 on the z axis from z = 0 to z = 10, with a groove round its side
from z = 4.2 to z = 5.8, 1.6 wide, cut 3 deep (to radius 7). Grow it by 1: the solid of every
point within 1 of the grooved drum.

Every point of the groove is within 0.8 of one of its walls, so growing fills it: its faces, moved
1 into the groove from each side, cross each other and are gone. The drum's top and bottom move
out to z = 11 and z = −1, its side to radius 11, and its two outer rims round to the outer quarter
of a torus's tube of radius 1. Where the groove was, the groove's two outer rims (the circles of
radius 10 at z = 4.2 and z = 5.8) become pieces of tori too, tubes of radius 1 about those
circles, running from the side at radius 11 inward and meeting each other in a sharp crease, the
circle of radius 10.6 at z = 5. So a shallow V-shaped notch, 0.4 deep, is all that is left of the
groove.

In a section through the axis, Q is the integral of the distance from the axis over the grown
drum with no groove, and M over the notch.
